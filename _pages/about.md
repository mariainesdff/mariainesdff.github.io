I am María Inés, a researcher (chargée de recherche) at CNRS, attached to the <a href="https://www.imj-prg.fr/" target="_blank">Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu-Paris Rive Gauche</a>.

Previously, I have held postdoctoral positions at the University of Bonn, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, and Imperial College London. Before joining Imperial College, I worked as an Assistant Professor (Profesor Ayudante Doctor) at Universidad Nebrija. I earned my PhD in Mathematics from Boston University in 2020, under the supervision of <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/jared-weinstein/home" target="_blank">Jared Weinstein</a>.

My main research interests are mathematical formalization and number theory.
